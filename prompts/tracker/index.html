How this board works
  1. The first tab, Most important first, ranks every prompt. What this brings on a card says what it would do for your planner, and Who does it says which kind of AI to send it to and how long it takes.
  2. Press Copy prompt and paste it into ChatGPT, Gemini, Muse, another Claude or any tool.
  3. Tap the tools you sent it to under Sent to. The Sent out tab then shows everything you sent, who has it and since when; tap a tool there to see only its prompts.
  4. When the answer comes back, press Add the answer (or open Answer and notes), paste it or attach the file, then save.
  5. Tick Done when you are finished with that prompt.

Claude copies every saved answer to the PC (into ai-responses/<prompt id>/), tries the good changes on a branch, runs the tests and reports back.
