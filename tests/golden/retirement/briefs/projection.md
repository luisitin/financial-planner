# Projection worker brief (lead)

README: "Yearly, in nominal dollars with a price level for the real view." Buckets: cash
"checking, savings, cash"; taxable "brokerage, crypto"; deferred "401(k), IRA, insurance cash value";
free "Roth, HSA". "Property, vehicles and 529s are left out."
"Working years: contributions grow with salaryGrowth until each person's retirement age. No withdrawals (pay covers life)."
"guaranteed income ... offsets it; the gap comes from cash, then taxable, then deferred (grossed up by the tax rate), then free."
"Returns: a stock/bond blend gliding from stocksNow to stocksRetired by the retirement year; a sequence option replaces the expected returns year by year"
"Success = the buckets cover the gap every year to the last adult's plan-end age. affordableSpending bisects the fixed-real spending."
"fixed-real (inflation-adjusted constant), percent of portfolio, guardrails ... cut 10% above 1.2x ... raise 10% below 0.8x ... no inflation raise after a losing year), VPW (pmt over the years left at the real return)."

Rules separately: readiness/primary selection; account buckets/sign/exclusions; spending source and
annualization; calendar retirement/end bounds including couple; contributions timing/growth;
income start/end/COLA/partner/typed SSA adapter; price level; glide/sequence; each spending strategy;
withdrawal ordering/flat gross-up and shortfall; RMD/surplus; post-flow growth and real balances;
affordable spending (independent closed form, whole-dollar rounding); coverage/verdict and summaries;
bracket taxation, gains/dividends, SSA income taxation, deductions/indexing, conversion eligibility.
The latter shared tax rules are inventoried/tested in withdrawal-order/roth-conversions briefs.
SSI formula itself is outside scope. RMD source gaps go to Pub 590-B. Bisection bound and
subcent settlement tolerances are implementation details, not an oracle: expect the mathematical
affordable amount and label disagreements if the bracket misses it. Strategy/income rounding is
only at CSV output. VPW uses end-period annuity by README; distinguish beginning-year timing issue.
Layout common contract: compute for adapter/summary; project for deterministic primitive schedules.

The completed key includes separate typed-benefit, partner-pay and RMD-surplus adapter
groups. Typed benefits are invented whole-dime amounts at full retirement age67, claimed
at67; their multiplication by12 tests integration without retesting the excluded SSA formula.
The partner's pay ends before their retirement age. The age75 surplus examples independently
use IRS Table III factor24.6 and deposit RMD less tax less spending into taxable.
