# Year by year worker brief (lead)

README: "Real (today's dollars) by default: nominal values divided by that year's price level."
Outputs quoted: "in (contributions, income, lines), out (spending, taxes), from (cash, taxable,
deferred, free), rmd, end buckets and total, shortfall; totals, retireYear, people, verdict, shortYear."
Rules: nominal ledger mapping; real deflation of every amount including income lines and
shortfall; totals across rows; age/retirement/inclusive horizon; empty/missing readiness.
Imported projection math is covered by projection.csv, not relabeled as new cash-flow math.
Use conservation of cash flows and geometric sums for independent crosschecks. No intermediate
cent rounding; cents only in expected CSV output. Layout common compute flattened plan/params.

The completed key adds nonzero contributions, every withdrawal bucket, tax and required
withdrawals in real mode. These cases distinguish an unscaled amount from a correct
division by the price level. IRS Table III factors25.5 at74 and24.6 at75 supplement the
projection assumptions; the birth cohort determines whether the age74 withdrawal applies.
