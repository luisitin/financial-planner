**The phone's Back often lands at the top of the page instead of where you were**

- pages: debt/payoff, retirement/projection, overview/dashboard, overview/pages, overview/plan
- reach: Every page reached again by Back or Forward.
- steps: The family's finished plan (verify/plan-done.json, made by walking the questions). - why: Missed by the walker. 'Do I land where I was' is one of the owner's four, and the answer here is 'sometimes'.
