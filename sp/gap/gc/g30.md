## Formulas (all in `compute.js`, pure)
- `w4Changes(d)`: the change and the earner it is on, exactly as the page chooses (typed suggestion on the first typed earner; else the estimate's main earner; filing separately each return's).
- `w4Sheet(plan, d, { today })`: Step 4(c) extra = the page's `perPaycheck` (and `addJanuary`'s January figure); a cut's Step 3 = `takeHomeMore`'s `step3` (or `january` when `one`); Step 3 plan figure = children under 17 x `childTaxCredit` for the year (2,200 for 2026) + other dependents x 500, 0 above `childTaxCreditPhaseOut` (200,000; 400,000 jointly; filing separately the household AGI against twice one return's line); "could go here" figure = round((plan Step 3 - Step 3 worked on) / paychecks a year) (Pub. 15-T divides Step 3 by pay periods); W-4P Step 2(b)(i) = yearly taxable pay from jobs (yours and a joint spouse's), (ii) = pensions paying less than this one, (iii) = sum; "No withholding" checked when the cut takes all of each payment and January's figure is at least a year of it.
- `formFor(plan, personId)`: "W-4P" when the earner has a pension and no salary on Income.
- `firstMove(..., pension)`, `step3Words(..., { pension })`, `addJanuaryWords(..., pension)`: W-4P and "payment" words; a job's output unchanged (byte-identical).

Sources read 2026-11-13 from the IRS forms themselves: 2026 Form W-4 (fw4.pdf, "Created 12/8/25": Step 3 $2,200 a child under 17 and $500 another dependent, income line $200,000 / $400,000; 2(c) "only two jobs total"; Step 4(b) worksheet 15 lines with tips 1a, overtime 1b, car-loan interest 1c, $6,000 at 65 3a/3b; standard deduction $32,200 / $24,150 / $16,100) and 2026 Form W-4P (fw4p.pdf, "Created 12/4/25": the "No withholding" box "I request that no withholding be withheld from my payments"; Step 2(b)(i)-(iii); Steps 3-4(b) only on the largest pension with (b)(i) blank; a separate W-4P per pension; a 17-line worksheet with the 65+ extra standard deduction). ## Builder chose (spec unclear)
1. **Steps 1 to 4(b) are "the W-4 the figure is worked on"**, not a fresh W-4 from the plan: the page's Step 4(c) is on top of what the paychecks take out now and a new W-4 replaces the old one, so filling Step 3 with the children while the figure was worked with Step 3 blank would leave the year short (85 a paycheck for the family). Estimated earners: the Paycheck page's W-4 (as typed there, else blank). Typed-from-a-stub earners and all pensions: "As on your W-4 now" with the plan's figure beside it, and "copy Steps 1 to 4(b) from the form you have now". The plan's Step 3 is still on the sheet, with what claiming it costs in Step 4(c).
2. The sheet is shown on screen as well as printed (a phone user reads it; printing from a phone is the rarer case).
3. "Each return" stays on the first view (the action), so the opt row's "The figures (each return)" became "4 more numbers" (the tiles).
4. Retirees with nothing withheld keep "Work out the payments"; the W-4P sheet appears once a pension's withholding is typed (the design names no pension figure for that case).
5. "Check" a box, not "tick" (tests/wording.test.mjs, US English).
6. A form's name never breaks at its hyphen (a scoped `white-space: nowrap` span in the sheet's own style).
