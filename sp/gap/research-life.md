## Mark-down: features this category should NOT attempt (or only as manual entry)
Anything flagged BANK or FEED (rent collection, bank feeds, credit-bureau scores and monitoring, live insurance quotes, live home values) conflicts with the app's "no bank logins or account numbers" promise; the app can instead take typed or pasted balances, files the person downloads, and self-entered score ranges. LIC rows (attorney review, CPA calls, superfunding, QCD) should become "ask a professional" notes, not features.

## Patterns worth copying (how products offer optional depth)
1. Free quick answer first, richer analysis behind a clearly labelled step (Undebt.it compare then custom; Holistiplan explain then scenario).
2. A single "extra payment" or "what if" slider on the first screen (Debt Payoff Planner, Credit Karma simulator).
3. Optional paper trail: printable tables, pre-filled forms (IRS W-4, Undebt.it+ prints).
4. Presets before free text: plan types as named choices (Undebt.it), term length suggestions (Policygenius).
5. Honest accuracy ladder: IRS estimator says paystub and last return make it more exact.
