What the words on this page mean
Page
One screen of the planner, such as Rental analysis or Employer stock. Each lives in its own folder with its math, its screen, its tests and a README.
New page
A page that went from a “coming soon” placeholder to working.
Answer key
A second, independent copy of a page’s money math, written only from the tax law and IRS publications, never from the app’s code. Thousands of made-up households are run through both; every place they disagree is a bug found, and the entry says what was fixed.
Lane
The set of pages one session owns, so two sessions never edit the same file at once. When a lane ran empty, its session took pages from idle lanes or shared work from the board.
Impact
A score out of 100 for how much an entry matters to someone using the planner. It adds three parts: reach, how much of the plan or how many households it touches (up to 35); size, how big the change is, such as a new page or a wrong number fixed (up to 40); and trust, how much more the numbers can now be relied on (up to 25). Every entry was scored, the scores were lined up against each other, and a second check moved eight that looked out of place; an opened entry says when that happened and why. Each one-line reason was then checked against the entry’s own details, and 28 were reworded to say only what the work shows.
Board update
A note on the shared work board (docs/WORKBOARD.md): claiming a page, releasing it, or a status line. Hidden by default; turn on the chip to see them.
claude-a, claude-b and the others
